## 4. Decision-Support Tool — NOT a Definitive Rule

Consistent with Mahajan (2024) §5.6 ("Addressing the Risks"), this software
is a decision-support tool, **not** a definitive rule. Output should always
be subject to expert review and adjustment based on the specific
circumstances of each weapon system, deployment scenario, and operational
environment. The H score is a baseline, not a verdict.

Treaty bodies, Article 36 weapons reviewers, procurement officers,
multidisciplinary expert panels, and NGO researchers using this tool are
encouraged to triangulate its outputs against domain expertise,
operational data, real-time intelligence, and the relevant legal framework
of the jurisdiction in which any decision will be made.

## 5. Dual-Use Disclosure

This software discusses autonomous weapon systems. Its purpose is to make
the cost of choosing less-safe oversight options visible, thereby promoting
greater human control in autonomous-systems deployment. The software is
**not** an autonomous-weapon-systems development kit; it does not contain
code, weights, models, or designs that contribute to the development or
manufacture of weapon systems themselves. It is a scoring instrument.

Users are responsible for verifying compliance with any applicable
export-control regimes in their jurisdiction (UK ECJU, US BIS,
Wassenaar Arrangement signatories, EU dual-use regulation) before
incorporating this software into any deployed system.
